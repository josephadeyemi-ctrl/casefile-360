# CASEFILE 360

## Cyber Incident Investigation, Response & Risk Platform

CASEFILE 360 is a cybersecurity investigation and response platform designed to demonstrate how SOC/DFIR, Security Engineering, Automation, and Governance, Risk & Compliance (GRC) teams can work together during a security incident.

The project uses a fictional organization, NexaCore Technologies, and synthetic security evidence to simulate a realistic enterprise incident investigation.

---

## Project Mission

CASEFILE 360 connects three cybersecurity perspectives:

### SOC / DFIR

**Question:** What happened?

The SOC/DFIR workstream investigates the incident, analyzes evidence, identifies indicators of compromise, reconstructs the attack timeline, and produces technical findings.

### Security Engineering

**Question:** How can we investigate and respond more efficiently?

The Engineering workstream builds CASEFILE 360 to collect, process, correlate, visualize, and manage investigation data.

### GRC

**Question:** What does the incident mean to the organization?

The GRC workstream evaluates business impact, security controls, risk, governance, and remediation requirements.
